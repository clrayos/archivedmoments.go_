privacy_

Last updated 28 Nov 2026
  1. Your photos stay on your phone.Taking, filtering and building your strip all happen on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
  2. We never see or keep your photos.There's no account, no cloud, no photo storage.
  3. You decide where they go.Photos only leave the app when you tap Save or Share, to the place you pick.
  4. The PRINTS counter is just a number.Each strip adds +1. No photos, names or device details.
  5. Simple counts, nothing about you.The app counts how many strips are saved, printed and shared, as plain numbers. No photos, names or phone details.
  6. Guestbook notes go to archivedmoments.pr.If you send one, it arrives privately by email, with your phone type and app version to help fix bugs.
  7. Fonts come from Google.Like most websites, the app loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google sees the page was opened. Never your photos.
  8. Questions?Message @archivedmoments.pr on Instagram.
